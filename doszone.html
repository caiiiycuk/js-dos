dos.zone

DOS.Zone is a js-dos community project. Its main goal is to build a repository of js-dos bundles that can be easily used to integrate DOS programs on any website.

DOS.Zone consists of:

  • discord, telegram for instant communication

  • twitter our news feed

  • dos.zone it is the default player for js-dos bundles based on js-dos api.
